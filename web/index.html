About Salon Back Office

Salon Back Office is a TokenMax burn-pool demo inspired by @WetterBo’s hair-salon back office — a warm, mobile-friendly desk for Miranda’s chair.

  • Week bookings calendar
  • Client cards with formulas + notes
  • Product inventory with low-stock flags
  • Revenue-by-week chart + receipt drawer

DEMO only. All clients, phones, emails, and receipts are fictional sample data. JT and Laney appear as first-name demo stand-ins (hair clients, not family). No real client PII is stored or invented as real.
